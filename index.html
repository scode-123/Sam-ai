Privacy & Security

Your privacy matters!

Deleting chats: Use Clear Saved Chats to delete saved conversations from this browser.

AI connection: Real AI, internet search and picture generation are not connected yet. This version does not send chat messages to an AI server.

Personal information: Do not enter passwords, financial details or other sensitive information.

Website code: The public website code can be viewed and copied by visitors.

Important: Clearing saved chats removes them from this browser's local storage. It does not delete copies someone may have made elsewhere.
